About Pagmamahal Maps

Last updated November 20, 2026

Not affiliated with Pag-IBIG Fund. Pagmamahal Maps is an independent, free website. It is not run, endorsed or checked by the Home Development Mutual Fund (Pag-IBIG Fund) or any government agency. To bid or buy, always use Pag-IBIG's official Online Public Auction.

What this site does

It shows Pag-IBIG's publicly listed acquired properties on one map, with search and filters, and offers a housing-loan calculator based on Pag-IBIG's published rates. Listings are refreshed once a day. Pag-IBIG auctions are sealed-bid, so only the minimum bid price is shown.

How accurate are the pins?

About a third of listings have a GPS reading from Pag-IBIG; those pins are exact. The rest are placed from the address: by other listings in the same subdivision, by the subdivision's location on open maps (usually within about 0.2–0.5 km), or by the barangay (within about 1–2 km). Each property shows how its pin was placed, with a shaded circle for approximate pins. You can fix a wrong pin with Pin in the wrong place?; corrections are checked automatically and by review before they appear.

Privacy

Pagmamahal Maps has no accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies. In line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), here is everything that is processed:

WhenWhatWhy, and who
Visiting the siteStandard web request data such as IP address and browser type Needed to deliver the pages and map. Handled by our hosting (Cloudflare) and map providers (OpenFreeMap, jsDelivr, cdnjs) under their own privacy policies. We don't keep these logs.
Your phone or browserA copy of the app and listings Lets the app open faster and work offline. Stored only on your device; clear it with your browser settings.
Reporting a wrong pinThe property ID, the spot you tapped, whether it applies to the whole building, the time, and a scrambled visitor code To check and apply the correction. The visitor code is a one-way scrambled value used only to spot repeat reports; your IP address itself is never stored by us. Reports are kept in the project's public GitHub repository so fixes can be reviewed and undone.
Bot check when reportingBrowser signals and IP address Cloudflare Turnstile uses these to tell people from bots, under Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Reports don't include your name, email or phone number, so we can't identify you from them. To ask about or remove a report, open an issue on the project's GitHub page with the property ID and the date you reported.
